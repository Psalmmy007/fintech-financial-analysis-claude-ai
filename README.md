Financial Performance & Transaction Analysis Using Claude AI

Project Overview

This project analyses 3,600 synthetic financial transactions from a fictional Nigerian fintech company.

The goal was to understand transaction performance, evaluate revenue and processing costs, identify profitable financial products, and recommend practical improvements.

I used Claude AI to assist with data analysis, financial reporting and visualisation, while independently checking calculations to ensure accuracy.

Tools Used

* Claude AI — AI-assisted financial analysis and reporting
* Microsoft Excel — Data validation, financial summaries and KPI reporting
* PowerPoint — Business presentation and visual communication

Dataset

The dataset contains 3,600 transactions across five financial products and six Nigerian regions.

It covers 18 complete months, from January 2025 to June 2026, plus 11 transactions dated 1 July 2026.

Monthly trend comparisons exclude the partial July period.

Note: All data is synthetic and does not represent a real financial institution.

Key Performance Indicators

Metric	Result

Fee revenue	₦1.52 million
Contribution	₦870,200
Contribution margin	57.3%
Transaction success rate	91.2%

Contribution represents fee revenue minus direct processing costs, not net profit.

Key Findings

1. Financial performance was stable rather than growing.

Monthly contribution ranged from approximately ₦38,900 to ₦55,300 across the 18 complete months.

2. Loan Repayments generated the highest contribution per transaction.

Loan Repayments generated approximately ₦524 per transaction, compared with ₦194 for Transfers.

3. Savings generated relatively low contribution.

Savings accounted for approximately 19.9% of transactions but only 5% of total contribution.

4. Transaction reliability needs attention.

Of 3,600 transactions, 3,284 were successful, 209 failed and 107 were reversed.

5. Lagos was the largest contributor.

Lagos generated approximately 41.5% of total contribution.

Business Recommendations

1. Review the profitability and strategic purpose of the Savings product before considering pricing changes.
2. Investigate failed and reversed transactions, particularly the October 2025 decline in transaction success rate.
3. Evaluate opportunities to grow Loan Repayments and Merchant Payments while considering customer demand, capacity and financial risk.

Data Validation

I independently reviewed AI-generated calculations and corrected discrepancies identified during the analysis.

The completed workbook documents 32 reconciliation checks covering transaction counts, values, revenue, costs and contribution across reporting summaries.
